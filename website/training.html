About this course

The EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) sets cybersecurity rules for connected products and software sold in the EU. Some duties already apply today, and the full requirements apply from December 2027.

This beginner course explains the CRA in plain language, then shows how it can be implemented step by step, using one fictional company as a running example.

You'll start with a short foundation: why the CRA exists, who has duties, which products are covered and how strict the rules are. Then you'll follow the implementation in two parts: the reporting duties that already apply, and the steps that bring a product to compliance — from classification and risk assessment to the security requirements, vulnerability handling and conformity.

Along the way, simple simulations, a workbook and short quizzes help you check your understanding.

What you'll learn

✔

Understand what the EU Cyber Resilience Act is, why it exists and the timeline every product team must plan for.

✔

Find out if the CRA applies to your product, and whether you are the manufacturer, importer, distributor or open-source steward.

✔

Classify your product as default, important or critical, and choose the right conformity route: self-assessment or notified body.

✔

Build the essential cybersecurity requirements into design and development, including vulnerability handling, SBOM and security updates.

✔

Report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents on time (24 h, 72 h, 14 days) through the EU single reporting platform.

✔

Take a realistic product from identification to CE marking, and finish with a checklist of every output you need for compliance.
